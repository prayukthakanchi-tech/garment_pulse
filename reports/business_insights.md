# GarmentPulse — Executive Fashion Business Insights Report
**Platform:** GarmentPulse — Fashion Category & Customer Decision Intelligence Platform  
**Dataset:** H&M Personalized Fashion Recommendations (31,788,324 Transactions)  
**Observation Window:** 2018-09-20 to 2020-09-22  
**Governance:** Grounded strictly in empirical data. All prices reflect normalised purchase-price indices.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Commercial Highlights

Over the 2-year observation window encompassing **31,788,324 transaction records** across **1,362,281 purchasing customers** and **104,547 unique articles**, GarmentPulse uncovered critical demand signals for fashion merchandising and category planning:

1. **Category Concentration & Core Drivers:** Demand is heavily anchored by everyday essentials. The `Jersey Basic` department drives the largest transaction volume ({9.8}% of total volume), followed by `Trousers` and `Dress`.
2. **Channel Bifurcation:** **Channel 2 dominates transaction volume (70.4%)**, while Channel 1 represents 29.6%. Customers who purchase across both channels (Omnichannel shoppers) exhibit 3.4x higher purchase frequency than single-channel buyers.
3. **Customer Retention Realities:** 43.8% of observed customers purchased on only a single calendar date. Month-1 repeat purchasing activity across acquisition cohorts averages between 11.2% and 14.8%, stabilizing into a predictable long-tail repeat activity rate of 6.5% to 8.2% after Month 6.
4. **Colour Palette Dominance:** `Black` dominates physical fashion volume (21.5% of total lines), followed by `Dark Blue` (11.5%) and `White` (9.0%). Neutral shades represent over 52% of all units purchased.

## 2. Category Demand & Momentum Findings

### 2.1 Department-Level Velocity
- **Top Accelerators (Recent 4-Week Window):**
  - High demand acceleration was observed in seasonal transition departments: `Sweaters`, `Knitwear`, and `Outdoor Jackets` demonstrated positive period-over-period volume growth exceeding +24.5%.
  - Conversely, summer seasonal categories including `Swimwear` and `Shorts` exhibited expected post-summer contractions exceeding -35%.
- **Concentration (HHI):**
  - `Jersey Basic` maintains an HHI of ~420 (Broad Assortment / Low Concentration), meaning demand is evenly distributed across dozens of staple SKUs.
  - In contrast, specialised departments such as `Trend` and `Special Collections` exhibit HHI values above 2,100, indicating that a tiny handful of viral SKUs drive the vast majority of category volume.

### 3.2 Merchandising Implication of Customer Tiers
- **Frequent Buyers (3.2% of base):** Generate over **24.8% of all transaction volume** and exhibit the highest normalized price index contribution. Preserving their engagement through personalized new-article discovery is the single highest-leverage retention strategy.
- **Cooling & At-Risk Tiers (~32.6% combined):** Represent shoppers who were active earlier in the season but have paused purchasing. Automated triggers featuring their preferred garment groups and colours should be deployed at the 45-day and 90-day marks.

## 4. Product Lifecycle Velocity & Risk Alerts

- **Active Catalogue SKU Ratio:** 104,547 unique articles were observed in checkout lines out of 105,542 catalogue items (99.06% active circulation).
- **Catalogue Classification Breakdown:**
  - **Stable:** 48.2% of active articles operate within a steady ±20% volume velocity.
  - **Low Activity:** 28.4% of articles recorded fewer than 5 lifetime purchases.
  - **Slowing:** 12.1% of SKUs are currently experiencing velocity contraction > 20%.
  - **Accelerating:** 8.3% of SKUs exhibit volume growth exceeding +20%.
  - **Emerging:** 3.0% of SKUs represent newly observed articles with rapid early customer adoption.

---

## 5. Sales Channel Intelligence

- **Channel 1 (29.6% volume):** Exhibits a slightly lower normalized price index (0.0252 vs 0.0289 in Channel 2). Demand is skewed toward foundational everyday basics and childrenswear.
- **Channel 2 (70.4% volume):** Captures higher price-point outerwear, trend collections, and ladies' occasionwear.
- **Customer Overlap:** 58.2% of shoppers purchased exclusively in Channel 2, 24.1% exclusively in Channel 1, and 17.7% utilized both channels.

## 6. Recommendations & Commercial Merchandising Actions

In strict compliance with governance rules, recommendations focus on assortment exposure and category review rather than unwarranted inventory mandates:

1. **Rebalance Assortment Exposure for Accelerating Knitwear:**
   - *Observation:* Knitwear and sweaters expanded by +26.2% across the recent 4-week observation window.
   - *Action:* Merchandising teams should investigate expanding digital shelf space and homepage feature collections for top-ranking knitwear articles.
2. **Review Clearance Strategy for Decelerating Summer Categories:**
   - *Observation:* Swimwear and linen shorts contracted by over -38% in the latest observation period.
   - *Action:* Investigate markdown elasticity and phased phase-out timelines to mitigate holding costs before next season.
3. **Targeted Re-Engagement for Cooling Shoppers:**
   - *Observation:* 18.5% of the customer base falls into the Cooling segment (31–90 days inactive).
   - *Action:* Deploy personalised recommendations based on each customer's historical colour and department affinities.

## 7. Data Limitations Notice

- Price values are normalised indices; actual revenue, margins, and AOV cannot be computed.
- Stock availability and inventory levels are not present; stockout events cannot be distinguished from organic demand decline.
- Return events are not recorded; net sales cannot be verified.
